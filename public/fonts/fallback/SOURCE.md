# Noto Serif fallback fonts

Regular and Bold are full static instances of Noto Serif 2.015. Italic and Bold Italic are full static instances of Noto Serif Italic 2.013. All use normal width (100), with weight 400 or 700. The SIL OFL 1.1 and copyright notice are in OFL.txt.

Pinned open sources:

- Normal: https://api.fontsource.org/v1/registry/sources/4d8e6761424656867019081a1a01336f3cb086982682698714054fc33f782713
- Italic: https://api.fontsource.org/v1/registry/sources/e87acbc6c0efd0d9a20d6a8cbbda2b266c14be3a3a6f5af8ec9d7b2460570ad1

Generated with fontTools 4.59.1 `instantiateVariableFont(TTFont(source), {"wght": weight, "wdth": 100}, updateFontNames=True)`. No glyph outlines were otherwise edited. These fonts are explicitly identified as replacements when exact-font recovery fails; they are not presented as Georgia or another original typeface. Full character coverage is embedded so a downloaded PDF does not reintroduce a subset missing uppercase or lowercase letters.
